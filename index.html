THE FIELD GUIDE

A lord's first orders

Grow a working population, keep popularity at fifty-five or higher, and defend your keep. Construction and recruitment costs appear in the ledger.

  1. Feed your people. Date orchards supply food directly. Wheat → mill → bakery makes bread. Hop farms → brewery supplies ale. Farms require fertile green ground.
  2. Rule the settlement. Town gardens improve popularity and troop damage while slowing production; gallows reverse the tradeoff. Select a workplace to pause it, releasing its workers and stopping its resource inputs and outputs.
  3. Arm the levy. Wood → fletcher → bows; iron → blacksmith → swords; iron → armorer → armor. Build the required recruiting building before raising troops. Mercenaries cost gold and need a mercenary post.
  4. Control fire. Slaves, fire throwers and pitch traps ignite flammable buildings. Fires burn for 24 seconds at 6 HP/s and spread across touching buildings, including friendly ones. Leave a full tile gap as a firebreak. Wells need two workers and suppress one nearby fire within seven tiles; staffing controls effectiveness. Range is abstract, without water hauling. Burning workplaces and recruitment stop; repair waits until the fire is out. Select a recruiting facility to set or clear its rally point.
  5. Build a fortress. Walls hold the enemy; gatehouses control passage. Towers fire automatically. Garrison ranged soldiers in towers for protection and range. Walls, closed gates, keeps and towers block ground-level ranged fire. Tower fire and shots to or from elevated garrisons clear low walls and gates; other keeps and towers still obstruct them. Ground shots at tower foundations remain blocked by walls and closed gates.
  6. Lead the siege. Select troops then right-click a hostile soldier or building to attack. Right-click ground to move. A then click orders an attack move; G then click a friendly tower garrisons. Catapults and trebuchets arc shots over fortifications; rams must reach their target.

Rival lords rebuild industries, buy supplies, repair safe buildings and defend their keep. The Emir balances growth and war; the Wolf raids exposed workshops early; the Warden garrisons towers and gathers larger, escorted expeditions.
